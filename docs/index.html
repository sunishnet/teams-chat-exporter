Your Teams chats, in formats you can keep.

Export 1:1, group, and meeting chats to readable HTML, CSV, and JSON. Sign in with Microsoft, choose the chats, and save a local copy.

Download for Windows Explore the source

Built with Rust and Microsoft Graph. Your exports are written to the folder you choose.

Three chat formats

Keep a styled transcript, open messages in a spreadsheet, or retain the raw Graph response.

🖼️

Useful details

Include replies, reactions, attachments, system events, participants, and optional pasted images.

🔒

Local exports

Sign in through Microsoft. Choose where exports are saved, and handle them as sensitive data.
