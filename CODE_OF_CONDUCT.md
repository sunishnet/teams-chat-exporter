# Community code of conduct

We want participation in Teams Chat Exporter to be respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the project.

Examples of behavior that supports this community include:

- Treating people with respect and patience.
- Giving clear, actionable feedback.
- Accepting responsibility for mistakes and correcting them.
- Respecting privacy by not posting chat exports, credentials, or private tenant data.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, and deliberate disclosure of private information are not acceptable. Project maintainers may edit or remove content and may restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub. Please include relevant links or context and avoid reposting sensitive information publicly.
